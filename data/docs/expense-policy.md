# Expense Policy

## Travel meals
The meal allowance on business travel is up to EUR 45 per day. Alcohol is not reimbursed.

## Receipts
Submit receipts in the expense tool within 30 days of the purchase. Late submissions require Finance Director approval.

## Approvals
- Expenses up to EUR 500: automatically approved if a receipt is attached.
- Expenses over EUR 500: require approval from your manager.
- Expenses over EUR 5,000: require approval from the Finance Director.

## Travel booking
Book flights through the company travel portal. Economy class for flights under 6 hours.
