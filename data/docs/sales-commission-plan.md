# Sales Commission Plan 2026 (CONFIDENTIAL)

- Base commission: 8% of new annual recurring revenue (ARR).
- Accelerator: 12% on all ARR above 120% of quarterly quota.
- Commission is paid monthly, one month in arrears.
- Clawback: commission is returned if the customer cancels within 90 days.

Questions go to the Head of Sales.
