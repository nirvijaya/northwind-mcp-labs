# Warehouse Safety Rules

## Personal protective equipment
- Hi-vis vests and safety shoes are required everywhere on the warehouse floor.
- Hard hats are required in the high-bay racking area.

## Forklifts
- Only certified operators may drive forklifts. Certification must be renewed every 3 years.
- Check the forklift before every shift and remove faulty forklifts from service immediately.

## Reporting incidents
Report every injury or near miss to your shift lead within 24 hours, and log it as a ticket.
