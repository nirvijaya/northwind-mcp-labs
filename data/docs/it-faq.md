# IT FAQ

## How do I connect to the VPN?
Install the NorthGate VPN client from the Self Service app and sign in with your Northwind account.

## How do I reset my password?
Go to it.northwind.example/reset and follow the steps. You need your MFA device.

## Is MFA required?
Yes. Multi-factor authentication is required for every account within 7 days of your start date.

## Who do I contact?
Create a ticket for the Operations department or message the IT Administrator.
