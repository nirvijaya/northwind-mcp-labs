# Northwind Logistics Employee Handbook

Welcome to Northwind Logistics. We move freight across the Nordics and the Baltics from our headquarters and main warehouse in Espoo.

## Working hours and time off
- Full-time employees work 37.5 hours per week.
- Every full-time employee receives 25 days of paid time off (PTO) per calendar year.
- Unused PTO up to 5 days can be carried into the next year.

## Code of conduct
Treat colleagues, customers and partners with respect. Report concerns to your manager or to the People team (HR).
