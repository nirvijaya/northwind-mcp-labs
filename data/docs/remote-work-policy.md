# Remote Work Policy

## Eligibility
All employees who have completed their first 30 days may work remotely. Warehouse roles are excluded because they require on-site presence.

## How much
- Employees may work remotely up to 3 days per week.
- Teams agree on at least one shared office day per week.

## Core hours
Everyone must be reachable during core hours, 10:00–15:00 Helsinki time, regardless of where they work.

## Equipment
Northwind provides a laptop and one external monitor. A home-office allowance of EUR 300 is available once every three years.
