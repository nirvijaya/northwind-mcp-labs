# Engineering Onboarding Guide

## Week 1
- Get your laptop and set up the dev environment with `nwctl setup`.
- Meet your onboarding buddy, assigned by your Engineering Manager.
- Ship your first pull request (a small fix or docs change).

## Week 2–4
- Shadow the on-call engineer for one shift.
- Read the Incident Response Runbook and Deploy Runbook.
- Complete the security awareness training.

## Who to ask
Your onboarding buddy first, then your Engineering Manager.
