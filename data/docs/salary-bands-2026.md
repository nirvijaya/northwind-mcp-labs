# Salary Bands 2026 (CONFIDENTIAL)

Annual base salary ranges in EUR.

| Level              | Minimum | Maximum |
|--------------------|---------|---------|
| Software Engineer I  | 46,000 | 57,000 |
| Software Engineer II | 58,000 | 72,000 |
| Senior Engineer      | 73,000 | 92,000 |
| Accountant           | 44,000 | 55,000 |
| Account Executive    | 42,000 | 52,000 (+ commission) |
| Warehouse Lead       | 38,000 | 46,000 |

Bands are reviewed every October. Offers above band maximum require Head of People approval.
